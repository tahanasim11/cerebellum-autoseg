Cerebellum AutoSeg

A rough cerebellum mask for every MRI, made automatically on your own computer, ready for you to check and fix in ITK-SNAP. No coding needed: you only double-click files.

Your scans never leave this computer. The setup downloads software from the internet once. After that, all processing runs locally, and it works offline.

1. What this does

For each MRI you pick, the kit:

  1. runs SynthSeg-robust 2.0, a published, validated brain segmentation model that works on any MRI contrast and resolution and is built to cope with clinical scans;
  2. takes the four cerebellum labels it produces (left and right cerebellar cortex and white matter) and merges them into one label (Label 1);
  3. saves that mask on exactly the same grid as your scan, so it lines up perfectly when you open it in ITK-SNAP.

You then review the mask and correct it, which should be much faster than tracing from scratch. The mask is a draft, not a final answer. Every mask still needs your review.

Step 1One-time setup

  1. Open the CerebellumAutoSeg folder (wherever you saved or extracted it).
  2. Double-click 1_SETUP (run once).bat. A black window opens.
  3. If Windows shows a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. This appears for any new script file and is expected. (If you downloaded the kit as a ZIP, you can avoid this by right-clicking the ZIP before extracting, choosing Properties, ticking Unblock and clicking OK.)
  4. Wait. You will see steps [1/5] to [5/5]. Step 3 (TensorFlow) is the slowest. The whole setup usually takes 5 to 20 minutes.
  5. When you see SETUP COMPLETE, press any key to close the window.

If anything fails (for example the internet drops), just double-click the setup file again. Finished steps are skipped, so it continues where it stopped.

Step 2Segment a scan

  1. Double-click 2_RUN on my scans.bat.
  2. A file window opens. Go to your scan and select the MRI itself (the .nii.gz file), not a segmentation. To do several scans in one go, hold Ctrl while clicking them.
  3. Click Open and wait. Each scan usually takes 1 to 10 minutes. You will see "SynthSeg pass 1 is running". Sometimes a second pass runs; that is normal (it re-centres on the cerebellum).
  4. When it finishes, it prints where the mask was saved, for example:
    Saved: ...\Downloads\patient01_T1_cerebellum_auto.nii.gz
  5. It asks "Open the scan and its new mask in ITK-SNAP now?". Press Enter (or type Y) to open both in ITK-SNAP automatically.

Shortcut: you can also drag one or more scan files onto 2_RUN on my scans.bat instead of using the file window.

Step 3Review and fix in ITK-SNAP

If ITK-SNAP did not open automatically: File > Open Main Image and choose the scan, then Segmentation > Open Segmentation and choose the ..._cerebellum_auto.nii.gz file. The cerebellum appears in red (Label 1).

A simple review routine

  1. Scroll through the axial view from the top of the cerebellum to the bottom, slice by slice (mouse wheel, or the Up / Down arrow keys).
  2. Then check the sagittal and coronal views, especially the midline.
  3. In the 3D view (click Update there if it is shown), look at the mask as a 3D shape. Holes, spikes or odd bumps stand out there.
  4. Use the label opacity slider to make the red mask more see-through when judging edges.

How to fix mistakes

  • Choose the Paintbrush tool from the main toolbar. Make sure the active label is Label 1.
  • Left-click and drag to add cerebellum. Right-click and drag to erase.
  • Change the brush size in the Paintbrush inspector on the left. For long edges, the Polygon tool is often faster.

Where the draft is most often wrong

AreaWhat to look for
Brainstem border (cerebellar peduncles)Mask creeping into the pons or medulla, or stopping short.
Top surface (tentorium)Leaks into the occipital lobe above, or missing the top of the cerebellum.
Bottom (tonsils, near the foramen magnum)Tonsils cut off or extended too low.
Flocculus (small lobule beside the brainstem)Often missed or partly included.
Fourth ventricleThe model leaves it out of the cerebellum. Add it if your protocol includes it.
Nearby veins and sinusesBright vessels next to the cerebellum wrongly included.
Abnormal anatomy (lesions, malformations, very young children)Check the whole mask carefully. These cases need the most correction.

Save your corrected mask

Segmentation > Save Segmentation Image As... and give it a new name, for example patient01_T1_cerebellum_corrected.nii.gz. Keep the _auto file unchanged. Using the same naming pattern every time will make it easy to train a custom model on your corrected masks later.

Step 4Test it against your manual segmentation

This tells you how close the automatic draft is to your own careful work, using a scan you have already segmented by hand.

  1. First run Step 2 on that scan, so its _cerebellum_auto.nii.gz file exists.
  2. Double-click 3_COMPARE with my manual segmentation.bat.
  3. Window 1: pick the MRI scan. Its auto mask is found automatically.
  4. Window 2: pick your manual segmentation of that scan.
  5. Read the results in the black window. They are also saved to CerebellumAutoSeg_comparisons.csv next to the scan.
  6. Press Enter to open the scan with a colour-coded difference map in ITK-SNAP.

Reading the numbers

MeasureMeaning
Dice overlap0 to 1. Above 0.90 means the draft is very close to your manual work. 0.85 to 0.90 usually means a consistent boundary difference worth looking at.
Auto volume vs manual (%)Positive: the draft is bigger than yours. Negative: smaller.
Mean surface distanceHow far apart the two outlines are on average, in mm.
95% Hausdorff distanceThe size of the worst disagreements, in mm (ignoring the most extreme 5%).

Reading the difference map

Red = both agree    Green = only the auto mask (extra)    Blue = only your manual mask (missed by auto)

Optional: to see these names in ITK-SNAP, use Segmentation > Import Label Descriptions and pick difference_map_labels.txt from the same folder.

If the green or blue areas follow a consistent pattern (for example the draft always leaves out the fourth ventricle, which your protocol includes), share the comparison numbers and a description of the pattern with whoever is helping you. Do not share the scans themselves. The kit can be adjusted to match your protocol.

7. A good first session

  1. Run Step 1 (setup) once.
  2. Pick one scan you have already segmented by hand and one you have not. Run Step 2 and select both at once (hold Ctrl).
  3. On the scan you already segmented: run Step 4 to see how close the draft is to your work.
  4. On the new scan: do Step 3. Review, fix and save as ..._cerebellum_corrected.nii.gz. Note roughly how long the fixing took compared with tracing from scratch.

8. Running many scans later

  • In Step 2, select all scans in a folder at once (click one, then press Ctrl+A). They are processed one after another; you can leave the computer running.
  • If one scan fails, the others still run. The window tells you which one failed and why.
  • A log, CerebellumAutoSeg_log.csv, records every scan, its draft volume and any warnings. Open it in Excel.
  • Once you have about 20 to 50 corrected masks, they can be used to train a model that learns your exact protocol (nnU-Net), so later drafts need even fewer fixes.

10. Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
"Windows protected your PC"Click More info, then Run anyway.
Setup says a download failedRun setup again. On a university network, a VPN or firewall can block downloads; try again on a different network if it keeps failing.
Setup check mentions "DLL" or "Visual C++"Ask IT to install the free Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable (x64), then run setup again.
"Setup has not been run yet"Run 1_SETUP (run once).bat first, and wait for SETUP COMPLETE.
"ran out of memory" or the run stops suddenlyClose browsers, Teams and Outlook, then run again. The kit automatically uses less memory on computers with less than 11 GB.
"This file looks like a segmentation"You picked a mask instead of the MRI. Pick the scan itself.
"did not find any cerebellum"Check that the scan is a brain MRI and includes the back of the head (posterior fossa).
Warning: "the cerebellum reaches the edge"The scan may cut off part of the cerebellum. Check those edges carefully.
ITK-SNAP does not open automaticallyOpen it yourself and load the files as described in Step 3.
ITK-SNAP says the segmentation size does not matchMake sure you opened the matching scan. The mask is always made on the grid of the scan you selected.
No file window appearsDrag the scan files onto 2_RUN on my scans.bat instead.

When asking for help, send a screenshot of the black window. If needed, also send the newest file from C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\CerebSeg\logs. Logs contain file names and technical messages, never image data.

11. Limits and how to describe this in a paper

  • Research use only. Not for clinical decisions.
  • SynthSeg's cerebellum is cerebellar cortex plus cerebellar white matter. It excludes the brainstem and the fourth ventricle. Your lab's definition may differ; Step 4 shows how much.
  • SynthSeg was trained on adult anatomy but is designed to be robust to contrast, resolution and scanner differences. It often works well in children, but expect more corrections in very young children and in abnormal anatomy. That is why every mask is reviewed.
  • All masks used in analyses should be the reviewed, corrected versions.

Suggested methods wording

"Cerebellar masks were pre-segmented automatically with SynthSeg-robust 2.0 (Billot et al., 2023), merging the left and right cerebellar cortex and white matter labels, and resampled to native image space. Every mask was then reviewed and manually corrected in ITK-SNAP by a trained rater."

References

  • Billot B, Greve DN, Puonti O, et al. SynthSeg: Segmentation of brain MRI scans of any contrast and resolution without retraining. Medical Image Analysis. 2023;86:102789.
  • Billot B, Magdamo C, Cheng Y, Arnold SE, Das S, Iglesias JE. Robust machine learning segmentation for large-scale analysis of heterogeneous clinical brain MRI datasets. PNAS. 2023;120(9):e2216399120.

12. Uninstalling

Delete the folder C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\CerebSeg (paste %LOCALAPPDATA% into the File Explorer address bar to get there) and the CerebellumAutoSeg kit folder. Nothing else was installed or changed. Your masks and logs next to your scans stay where they are.
